Creative Audit — brutally honest read on the current site

What works

Black/red is genuinely unclaimed territory in food. The performance-nutrition angle is a real differentiator. The technical foundation is fast and clean.

Outdated

Rounded cards, soft shadows, centered hero, one accent color — reads as 2019 SaaS dashboard, not a food brand.

Generic

Icon+number metadata, single typeface, stock-style photography with no consistent art direction. Nothing here has a point of view.

Hurts trust

Bare macro numbers with no methodology signal. No authorial voice — "Dudu's Kitchen" should feel like someone's kitchen, not a database.

Hurts engagement

Filter → grid → modal is purely transactional. No emotional arc, no shareable moment, no sense of time or season.

Missed opportunity

"Discipline meets appetite" is unclaimed — most healthy-eating brands are soft and pastel. No ritual, no manifesto, no recurring visual signature.

Path to iconic

One disciplined motif repeated everywhere (a red thread, a seal, an enso) plus a real editorial voice and an unmistakable type pairing.
