Cinnamon Banana Protein Pancakes

Blended oat pancakes with banana, eggs and whey, served with cool yogurt and berries.

410kcal
35gprotein
42gcarbs
20min
Ingredients
  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 1 small banana
  • 2 eggs
  • 20 g vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 100 g plain skyr and berries, to serve
Method
  1. 1Blend the oats, banana, eggs, protein powder, baking powder and cinnamon.
  2. 2Cook small rounds in a non-stick pan for 1-2 minutes per side.
  3. 3Stack with skyr and fresh berries.
