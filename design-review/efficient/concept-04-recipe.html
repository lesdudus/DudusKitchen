Berry Protein Overnight Oats

Creamy oats with skyr, berries, chia and vanilla protein for a fast post-workout breakfast.

390kcal
33gprotein
48gcarbs
10min
Ingredients
  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 170 g plain skyr
  • 100 ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 20 g vanilla protein powder
  • 80 g mixed berries
  • 8 g chia seeds
  • Cinnamon to taste
Method
  1. 1Stir the oats, skyr, milk, protein powder, chia and cinnamon until smooth.
  2. 2Cover and chill overnight, then loosen with a splash of milk if needed.
  3. 3Finish with the berries just before eating.
