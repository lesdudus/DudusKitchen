Berry Protein Overnight Oats

Sumo Ring · 390 kcal33g protein48g carbs10 min1 serving

Method

  1. Stir the oats, skyr, milk, protein powder, chia and cinnamon until smooth.
  2. Cover and chill overnight, then loosen with a splash of milk if needed.
  3. Finish with the berries just before eating.

Ingredients

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 170 g plain skyr
  • 100 ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 20 g vanilla protein powder
  • 80 g mixed berries
  • 8 g chia seeds
  • Cinnamon to taste
